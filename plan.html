How does the Spotify Premium trial work?

New Premium users can try Individual free for 1 month. After the trial ends you'll be charged ₦1,600 per month, unless you cancel before the trial is over.

How do I cancel my Premium plan?

You can cancel at any time from your account page. You'll keep your Premium benefits until the end of the period you've already paid for.

How does the Premium Duo plan work?

Premium Duo is for two people who live at the same address. Each person gets their own Premium account and their own listening.

How does the Premium Family plan work?

Premium Family covers up to 6 people living at the same address. The plan manager gets parental controls for the family members on the plan.

How does the Premium Student Plan work?

Students enrolled at an eligible accredited institution of higher education get a discounted rate of ₦800 per month, after 1 month free for new Premium users.

How much is Spotify Premium in Nigeria?

Individual is ₦1,600 per month, Student is ₦800 per month, Duo is ₦2,100 per month and Family is ₦2,500 per month.
